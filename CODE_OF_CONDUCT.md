# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful and harassment-free.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Respect different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Violations should be reported to the project maintainer. Maintainers may remove comments, block users, or reject contributions that violate this code.
